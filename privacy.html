Privacy Policy

Thermopylae: Last Spartan · Effective 22 July 2026

The short version

This game collects no data. None. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The app works entirely offline.

What stays on your device

  • Your best kill count, career kill count, and settings (such as sound on/off) are stored locally on your device and never leave it.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

Sharing clips and score cards

The game can record a short replay of your final moments and compose a score card image. Recording happens entirely on your device using Apple's ReplayKit. Nothing is uploaded anywhere by the game — a clip or image only leaves your device if you choose to share or save it through the iOS share sheet, and then only to the destination you pick.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game submits your kill counts to Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the developer does not receive, store, or process any Game Center account information. You can disable Game Center for this game in iOS Settings, and the game works fully without it.

Children

The game is rated for mature audiences due to intense stylized violence and is not directed at children. Since no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

Changes

If a future update ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated here first, with the effective date revised.
