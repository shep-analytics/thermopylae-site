THERMOPYLAE

Last Spartan · Molon Labe

A brutal, skill-based endless duel at the Hot Gates. One Spartan, one spear, one hit — theirs or yours. Every enemy telegraphs, every death is your fault, and the kill counter is the only thing that matters. For iPhone.

Support

Common questions

How do I play? Hold the left side of the screen to move. Flick up to jump, pull down to shield. Tap the right side to stab, tap twice to throw the spear, tap in the air to plunge. Every blow kills — theirs and yours.

Where is my data stored? Entirely on your device. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and collects nothing. See the privacy policy.

Can I turn the sound off? Yes — the speaker button on the title screen or pause menu.
